# MarketPrice design system

Source guidance: UI/UX Pro Max by NextLevelBuilder (MIT), https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill . Installed locally in the workspace; no private data supplied to its queries. Verified style result: Minimalism & Swiss Style, full RTL support, low performance cost. The generated enterprise landing-page pattern/dark palette was inappropriate for this existing pricing workspace and was not adopted.

- Audience: seller/admin reviewing a cost spreadsheet and promotion decisions.
- Surface: light grey canvas, white cards, restrained blue actions, dark text; local Tahoma/Segoe UI/Arial fonts for Persian/English without external font calls.
- Density: compact review workspace with progressive disclosure. Core flow: file → sheet/units → mappings → calculation → corrections/scenarios → evidence review → export.
- Motion: subtle180–200ms hover/reveal, no continuous decoration; reduced-motion disables animations and transitions. Visible keyboard focus and44px action targets.
- Tables: preserve units and identifiers; one plain-language decision per product; detailed evidence belongs under audit/competitor review.
- Honesty: marketplace labels are mapping support. Cached formula values and unmodeled price-dependent fees require review. No implied BuyBox guarantee or verified seller contract.
- Privacy: no merchant workbook, results or screenshots sent to third-party design tools or public repository. Screenshot publication explicitly declined by user.
