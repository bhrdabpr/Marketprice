# Demonstration and launch materials

## Product description

MarketPrice helps marketplace sellers understand which prices cover costs and whether discounts preserve contribution margin. It turns a product workbook into explainable price floors, campaign scenarios and a traceable audit workbook, while refusing to treat unknown costs as zero.

## 90-second demonstration

1. Show the actual app landing screen and sample download.
2. Open the sample: a USD product, a Persian TOMAN product and one intentionally missing shipping value.
3. Upload it through the UI. Explain explicit zero versus blank.
4. Calculate; show two valid products and one retained error.
5. Open Recommendations: the golden product's 20% margin floor is 117.65 USD.
6. Compare seller-funded and shared-funded campaigns; point out that unsafe overrides remain unsafe.
7. Download the workbook and inspect Errors, assumptions, original inputs and versioned rules.
8. Close with the distinction between a cost floor and demand-based market optimization, and the current rule/deployment limits.
